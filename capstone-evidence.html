1. Project and URLs

Project, audience, and purpose
Fillmore Jazz Years is a short public history site about the jazz scene in San Francisco's Fillmore District from the 1940s to the 1960s. It's for Bay Area residents, jazz fans, students, and families with ties to the neighborhood. It explains how the scene was built and cleared, gives a quick reference to the clubs, and lets readers request a guided tour past the former club sites.
Priority tasks
  1. Read how the scene grew and how redevelopment cleared it.
  2. Look up a club, where it stood, when it ran, and what it was known for.
  3. Request a walking tour of the former club sites.

4. Three Findings and Repairs

Finding and condition Impact Repair Exact retest and result
1. Menu never closes. At 375px on the live site, clicking the Menu toggled aria-expanded but the list stayed open. Every page at narrow widths: the nav list always takes over the top of the page, and the button state doesn't match what's shown. The CSS Checked [data-enhance], but navigation.js sets data-enhanced. Realized that it was a typo in styles.css. At 375px: the list starts closed, Menu open and closes it. Escape closes it with focus back on the Menu. Pass after repair.
2. Nav text disappears on hover. At 1280px, hovering the nav link turned its background white behind white text. Mouse users lose the label of the link they're pointing at in the main navigation. Hover backlground was --color-on-action which is (white) instead of --color-action Which is a (plum color) Hovering each nav link at 1280px and Menu at 375px: now have a white underline text on a plum background color. Pass after repair.
3. Character limit not enforced.Ran the html validator on tour.html: max-length not allowed on textarea. As a result you could enter 300+ characters into Access needs and it was accepted. The help text promised a 300 character limit but the form didn't enforce it, and the page ended up failing validation. Renamed max-length to maxlength. After repair html validator showed 0 errors. Was able to get stopped at 300 character limit. Pass after repair.

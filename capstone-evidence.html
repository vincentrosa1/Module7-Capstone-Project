1. Project and URLs

Project, audience, and purpose
Fillmore Jazz Years is a short public history site about the jazz scene in San Francisco's Fillmore District from the 1940s to the 1960s. It's for Bay Area residents, jazz fans, students, and families with ties to the neighborhood. It explains how the scene was built and cleared, gives a quick reference to the clubs, and lets readers request a guided tour past the former club sites.
Priority tasks
  1. Read how the scene grew and how redevopment cleared it.
  2. Look up a club, where it stood, when it ran, and what it was know for.
  3. Request a walking tour of the former club sites.
